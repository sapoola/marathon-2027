## 4. Session notes

- **Base phase quality is strides and fartlek only** until B7. Your aerobic system doesn't need intervals yet; your calves and hamstrings need weeks of easy running first. (You tagged "hamstring 🤝 tendons" on a run last October — that's the thing we're avoiding.)
- **Easy means under HR 145.** Your recent runs at 5:30–6:00 look right. Don't let the cyclist's engine drag the pace down to 5:10 on easy days — the legs aren't ready even though the lungs are.
- **Long run:** easy pace, walk breaks fine in base. MP blocks go at 5:15–5:20 on the nose.
- **Threshold:** comfortably hard, 4:40–4:48. Cruise intervals, not one long tempo.
- **Strides:** 6 × 20–25 s after easy runs from B2. Cheap, and they keep turnover while everything else is slow.

## 7. Rules for when the plan meets reality

- **Missed a session:** skip it. Never double up.
- **Tuesday bike left you flat on Wednesday:** run Wednesday easy with strides, do the quality session Friday instead of the easy run, and make Wednesday's legs light. Once is fine; twice in a row means the Tuesday session is too big for this phase.
- **Anything in the calf, Achilles or hamstring that changes your stride:** stop, ride instead, reassess in 48h. Two weeks of caution in September costs nothing; a tendon in December costs the race.
- **Priority when something has to go:** Saturday brick → Saturday ride length → Friday easy run → Tuesday swim → gym upper body → Thursday bike. The Tuesday hard bike goes before the Wednesday run does. The quality run and long run only go in cutback weeks.
- **Cycling creep is the biggest risk to this plan.** If a week's bike time is above the cap, that week's long run gets cut by 20%. Check Strava weekly totals on Saturday night.
- **Volume feels easy by W7:** add 2 km to Wednesday. Don't add intensity, don't add a sixth run.
